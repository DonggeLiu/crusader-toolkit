# Artful Dodger

Your horse archers sidestep arrows, bolts and stones, and take enemy towers without losses.

- Select horse archers and press Numpad `.` to toggle dodging for them.
- Dodging horse archers stand and shoot as usual; they move only when an enemy shot is about to land on them, then return to where you sent them.
- Watches arrows, crossbow bolts (including from towers), stones, ballista bolts, sling stones, fire pots and javelins; tested against archers, crossbowmen, mangonels and ballistas.
- Dodge spots keep the enemy in range, keep a safe distance from shooters and avoid crowding.
- Optional kiting: back away from approaching melee enemies while staying in range.
- Tests (10 horse archers): open-field hit rate about 95% → under 2%. Against a tower with 10 archers and 5 crossbowmen, covered by two more garrisoned towers: standing still, all died with no kills; dodging, the tower was cleared with no losses.

Mod Options → Artful Dodger: master switch, kite melee, auto-enable for new horse archers. Key: `[General] ToggleKey` in the config. Horse archers only; fire on the ground, braziers and boiling oil are not dodged.

Designed for single-player. Multiplayer is untested and may cause desyncs; use it there at your own risk.

Requires Script Extender 2.10.1+ and its BepInEx Loader (install the loader first). Subscribe on the Workshop and restart when prompted. Manual install: merge the ZIP's BepInEx folder into the game folder.

[Script Extender](https://gitlab.com/rawra-stronghold-crusader/shcde-script-extender/-/releases)

Mod Options → Artful Dodger · `BepInEx/config/SmartDodge_Dongge.cfg`

Chinese game languages use Chinese text, others English; restart after changing the language.
