# Resource Layout

Plan farms and industry around limited resource areas.

- Shift+X plans farms; Shift+N plans quarries, iron mines or pitch rigs under the cursor.
- Choose farm types with the icons. Shift+1…4 increases the minimum; Alt+Shift+1…4 decreases it.
- Left-click pins a layout; right-click cancels and clears pinned layouts. Double-click a pinned ghost to build in single-player, with normal placement checks and costs.
- The solver distinguishes proven optimal layouts from the best found before its time limit. Minimum counts may be impossible to fit.
- Quarry plans include estimated ox-tether positions. Distance, congestion and worker access can affect actual results. Pinned layouts reset on new game/load.

Enabled by default. General.TimeLimitMs defaults to 5000. FarmSelection and FarmMinimums remember your choices. Debug.DebugTools defaults to false; developer build/delete shortcuts are not normal gameplay controls. Direct ghost construction and developer tools are disabled in multiplayer; planning is multiplayer untested.

Direct ghost construction and developer tools are single-player only. Multiplayer planning untested.

Requires Script Extender 2.10.1+ and BepInEx Loader (install loader first). Subscribe and restart when prompted. Manual install: merge the ZIP’s BepInEx folder into the game folder; move an old short-name plugin folder outside plugins first, preserving config.
