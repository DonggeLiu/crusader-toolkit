# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Health bars are also colored and shown in the map editor (`ShowInMapEditor`, on by default).
- Shield-color mode (`ColorMode = Shield`, Mod Options): every other player's bar uses their shield color, read from the game's player palette; your own stay green, and a player with a green shield borrows your shield color.
- Lords' bars are 1.2x larger with a gold crown on top, drawn from the game's own crown emblem (`MarkLord`, on by default).
- Recoloring now applies the target color's brightness too, so light colors no longer wash out.
- 地图编辑器里也显示并着色血条（`ShowInMapEditor`，默认开启）。
- 盾牌色模式（`ColorMode = Shield`，可在 Mod Options 勾选）：其他玩家的血条用各自的盾牌颜色（取自游戏的玩家调色板），己方仍为绿色；盾牌为绿色的玩家改用你的盾牌颜色。
- 领主血条放大 1.2 倍，上方戴金色皇冠（取自游戏自带的皇冠徽章，`MarkLord`，默认开启）。
- 着色时目标颜色的亮度也生效，浅色不再发白。
