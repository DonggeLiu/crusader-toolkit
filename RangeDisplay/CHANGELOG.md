# Changelog

## 1.4.0

- Share optional player shield colors from Health Bar Colors, including when bar recoloring is disabled.
- 支持血条颜色的可选盾牌配色，关闭血条着色时仍共享配置。

- Preview tower ranges while placing a tower: the archer range as a dashed ring; on Square and Round Towers also the mangonel (inner) and ballista (outer) ranges as long dashes. The rings cover every tile of the tower top, and a semi-transparent archer, mangonel or ballista icon on each ring shows whose range it is. New setting, on by default.
- Show the Healer's search range as a dotted ring: 4 / 7 / 10 tiles by stance. A Healer on a tower outlines the tower top instead. New setting, on by default.
- Use the game's official names for siege engines and units; add the missing mangonel to the settings and descriptions.
